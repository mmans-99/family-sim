# Vision

A "self-running" house containing a simulated family who live their own lives.
You watch; you don't control (at least not by default).

## The family (start)
Mum, Dad, one school-age child, one toddler. See `FAMILY.md`.

## What they must have
- Their own needs (hunger, energy, hygiene, fun, social, comfort, bladder)
- Personality, moods, emotions, memories, relationships with each other
- Jobs / school / nursery, money, bills
- Free will: they choose what to do, including leaving the house
- Lives that move forward: ageing, growing up, careers, partners, marriage,
  children of their own, eventually old age and death
- Reactions to the outside world: weather, seasons, the economy, random events

## What the player sees
- **Inside the house:** a visual 2D house; people walk around, use objects,
  with an icon/bubble showing what they're doing and how they feel.
- **Outside the house:** a running text commentary ("Dad is stuck in traffic
  on the way to work, irritated").
- A timeline/diary per person, and a family tree.
- Clock + speed controls.

## Scope rule
The house is the whole world for now. The outside world is simulated as
text and numbers only (no visual town). The view draws a thin edge of scenery
around the house (pavement, road, the neighbouring half of the semi, fences)
so it does not float on blank space; nothing happens there. A visible street
or neighbourhood may come in Phase 3, when people leave the house; this rule
will be revisited then (DECISIONS 2026-11-11).

## Not in scope (for now)
- Player building/buying furniture
- A visual town
- Online multiplayer
- Paid AI / LLM dependency
